# X03 TradeCommand and approval local admission

`AtomicCommandIssuer` in `quantos-execution::authority` is the X03 keyed authority. The legacy root `CommandIssuer`/`ApprovalRecord`/`TradeCommand` are fixture compatibility APIs. Their public checksums are not authority and cannot be submitted through this API.

The backend constructs one RiskGuard and one issuer per tenant/workspace and shares their clones. Register an R04 repository record and exact TradeIntent using its authenticated originator. For an approval-required intent, request approval, apply a scoped non-self approver's CAS transition, obtain the stored token, and evaluate X02 with that token. Pass the resulting Pre Allow and freshly resolved X01/S03/financial/venue sources to `issue`. An Allow below the approval threshold may issue without an approval; missing approval above threshold refuses. The backend owns identity, record provenance, current source facts and clock. Never expose these trusted inputs as unchecked client assertions.

Issuer -> risk is the sole lock order. Every issue/replay reruns current risk under the kill mutex, then atomically signs, consumes approval and stores the idempotent command/audit. A failed attempt consumes nothing. An exact replay returns the same signed command and records replay; another issuer sharing the guard cannot consume its risk decision. Requester/proposal/decision/approval and venue conflicts refuse, as do stale sources, expiry, invalid release, missing approval or kill revision changes. Commands are short lived and cannot extend any proposal/risk/source/approval deadline. `verify_command` checks current local membership/signature/identity/lifetime/kill; it makes no downstream submission. TP07 must own dispatch, current-source/revocation checks and exactly-one downstream consumption.

Approval Pending -> Approved/Rejected/Cancelled and Approved -> Cancelled transitions use revision CAS. The requester alone cancels; requesters and proposal originators cannot approve/reject their own proposal. Consumed/Rejected/Cancelled never revive. Expiry is enforced on operations, while `approval_status` reports the last stored state. Successful transitions and issue/replay/refusal records form a complete signed local audit chain. Keys are injected and never logged/serialized.
